Support

Something not working, or have a question? Write to us at contacto@pepebits.com.

Before you write

  • The shortcut doesn’t respond: check in System Settings → Privacy & Security that SaySo has Microphone and Input Monitoring. In SaySo, the Dictation screen tells you what’s missing.
  • The agent doesn’t answer: check in SaySo → Model that your AI account has a key and credit, and in Home that the agent is running.
  • The Telegram bot doesn’t answer: in SaySo → Telegram, check that the bot is connected and paired with you.
  • Something failed: SaySo → Logs shows what happened and when.

Error report

In Settings → Error report you can prepare a report with the version, the system and the recent log, with keys masked. It’s a text file: review it and attach it to your email.
